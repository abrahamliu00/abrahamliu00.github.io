Privacy Policy

This notice describes how the current version of this personal website handles information about visits. I will update it if the site’s features or services change.

Visiting the Site

You do not need an account to browse the site. The hosting provider may process ordinary request data, such as your IP address, browser information, time of access, and requested page, to deliver the site, operate the service, and troubleshoot problems. How long this data is kept depends on the hosting provider selected for deployment.

Browser Preferences and Page Views

The site saves your language and theme preferences in your browser, and an analytics choice if a service is enabled. This GitHub Pages version has no site-operated pageview counter. You can clear local storage in your browser settings.

Third-Party Content

The site may load fonts from Google Fonts, and some images or embedded content may come from external services. Your browser requests that content directly from those providers. External sites linked here apply their own privacy practices when you visit them.

Analytics, Advertising, and Comments

If configured, this site loads Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity only after you agree. Declining prevents those scripts from loading. Advertising and comments are not enabled.

The homepage uses Statable to measure visits and show approximate visitor locations and activity in the last 30 minutes. Its official public widget appears only at the bottom of the homepage. The free plan’s analytics dashboard is public. Statable says it does not use cookies and respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. Your browser makes requests directly to Statable.
