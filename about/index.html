About

I’m Yuhan Liu. I’m interested in machine learning and statistics, particularly how models learn from data and how to make them reliable in practice.

I completed an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at The University of Hong Kong, after earning a BEng in Internet of Things Engineering at Hubei University. This site is where I document what I learn, build, and think through along the way.

What This Site Is

This is my personal site, where I bring together three kinds of work:

  • Projects: implementations, technical decisions, and lessons from building them.
  • Notes and reflections: questions and insights from studying machine learning, statistics, and related topics.
  • Research exploration: questions I’m interested in and results I can share when they are ready.

What I’m Exploring

I want to understand why models work, when they fail, and how to evaluate and apply them more reliably. Writing helps me make the questions, methods, and open ends clearer; projects give me a way to test ideas against practical constraints.
